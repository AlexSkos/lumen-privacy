Google and its advertising partners may process certain information automatically, including device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and information related to advertising performance.

Advertising data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policies and applicable privacy laws.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details, bank account information, or other payment credentials.
